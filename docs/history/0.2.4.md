### 29.2 Skills, talent and Persona states

Sources: the official CN `character_table`, `skill_table`, `battle_equip_table`, `uniequip_table`, and [PRTS 结城理](https://prts.wiki/w/%E7%BB%93%E5%9F%8E%E7%90%86) (talents, skill notes and PUM-Y notes).

- The existing dollkeeper profession owns the 20-second substitute timer, switching invulnerability / HP reset, block release, and return to the body. The selected skill determines the initial Persona: S1 Orpheus, S2 Thanatos, S3 Thanatos. Switching out ends the selected instant skill as it does in the official note.
- S1 Orpheus attacks for Arts damage, but when an operator strictly below 50% HP is in range, the attack heals the lowest-HP eligible operator for the skill's ATK scale instead. At exactly 50%, Orpheus attacks.
- S2 Thanatos attacks up to the official target count for Arts damage and rolls the official fear chance per normal hit. Its x-1 aura checks every 0.1 seconds, can hit air, and once per range entry applies the official 9,999,999 sourceless true-damage execution to feared non-character targets at or below the ATK threshold. Source/kill credit remains Makoto's. The exported enemy schema lacks a role-unit category; the kit excludes such units if future records supply a discriminator, otherwise it treats the simulated enemies as non-character units **[ASSUMED]**.
- Base S3 Thanatos receives the skill's ASPD and target cap; each physical / Arts hit is retyped as the damage type with greater DEF / RES mitigation, while ties retain the original Arts type. It switches to Orpheus on the second S3 activation or a fatal hit. Base Orpheus keeps attacking, restores the two blocks, grants the rank-based physical / Arts dodge to every eligible operator in range, and heals up to the rank-based target cap at once on entry and every second.
- PUM-Y stage 1 adds its official substitute HP increase. Stages 2 / 3 replace the talent-1 blackboard from the module's `talentChanges`. Stage 3's S3 starts as Thanatos·改, can hit air, and grants one no-SP S3 recast for Orpheus·改. The simulation's ordinary 3-second automatic-operation cooldown delays that recast beyond the official 1-second switch animation **[SIM ADAPTATION]**. Orpheus·改 has no normal attack, restores two blocks, grants the official dodge to all eligible operators, and applies the rank-based capped heal 0.5 seconds after entry and each one-second pulse.
- The second talent's S.E.E.S. total attack runs after any substitute ends, not only S3. Each deployed S.E.E.S. operator (and Makoto) hits all selectable ground / air enemies in that member's x-1 range for the talent's true-damage scale. Makoto enters the 1.6-second invulnerable / untargetable All-Out form first, then returns to the body.
